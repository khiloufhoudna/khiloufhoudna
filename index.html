Dz-Emotion: Dataset for Algerian Dialect Emotion Detection

Oct 2024 - Jan 2025

This project aims to collect a large dataset of Algerian dialect comments from social media platforms (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram). The comments will be annotated with the correct emotions, allowing for the creation of a comprehensive dataset. This dataset will serve as the foundation for building a large language model capable of accurately recognizing and processing emotions in the Algerian dialect. (Almost completed 6 labels, each with 1000 comments).

Dz-Corpus: A Large-scale corpus for the Algerian Dialect

Sep 2025 - Mar 2026

Dz-Corpus is a large-scale corpus under development for the Algerian dialect. The dataset is being collected from social media platforms such as YouTube. So far, more than 5 million YouTube comments have been collected to capture the natural usage of Algerian Arabic, including code-switching with French. The project's goal is to build the first large-scale dataset for the Algerian dialect and train a transformer-based model that can support NLP tasks such as sentiment analysis, emotion detection, and dialogue systems, contributing to low-resource language research.

DzVoice : A Low-Resource Algerian Dialect Speech Dataset

Apr 2026 - Present

DzVoice Corpus is a speech dataset developed for automatic speech recognition in the Algerian dialect, containing more than 3000 aligned audio and transcription pairs (until now). The dataset reflects real-world spoken language variability, including informal speech and code-switching. It aims to facilitate research on low-resource ASR by enabling the adaptation of pre-trained models and improving recognition performance on dialectal Arabic.
